## Beginner Explanation

### Follow the candidate from test to approval

A release candidate is a specific version proposed for use. Deployment installs or activates it in an environment. Release makes it available to users. The team can deploy internally first and later use a feature flag, a setting that controls access, to expose the same version to a small fraction of customers.

Open the green CI report and ask which version and cases it actually checked. If the report covers refund eligibility and order lookup, it tells you how those cases ran. You still need current authorization findings and a named person to approve exposing the candidate. It may miss traffic, tool behavior or policy changes outside that suite. Release readiness also depends on current findings, complete receipts, privacy checks, a functioning monitor and a way to contain or revert the change.

The release owner coordinates the decision. The approval authority is a named person whose assigned role permits the proposed scope. Northstar requires a separate reviewer. This separation of duties stops the person who submitted a high-risk change from being its only approver. A versioned release policy tells both people what to verify. The evidence package holds the CI report, manifest, findings, risk register, exact candidate and previous approved configuration, rollout and rollback plans and sign-off.

### Decide what remains unresolved

A critical finding is a confirmed defect with severe impact, such as an unauthorized financial write. It blocks this release even if the frozen cases all passed. Investigate why the finding and the green run coexist. A valid CI run can cover a different slice of behavior. Repair the cause, add the missing regression case and produce new evidence.

Suppose Northstar adds a human fallback for confusing refund questions. A customer can still wait longer for clarification. That delay is residual risk. The support owner records how the team will track it and when to review the acceptance. Document its impact, mitigation, owner, evidence and reassessment date. An accountable risk owner can accept a defined remaining risk within policy. An exception is a scoped deviation from a normal requirement. It moves from request to review and approval, then expiry and closure or renewal. Give it an ID, justification, approved version and scope, owner, approver, evidence and expiry. An expired exception cannot support a new decision. Neither ordinary risk acceptance nor an exception can waive Northstar’s critical authorization or privacy requirements.

| Pre-release decision | Northstar rule | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| INVALID EVIDENCE | Conflicting versions, identity, scope record or evaluation metadata make the packet unreliable | Repair the packet before comparing |
| BLOCK | A valid packet reports a failed CI or critical check, unresolved non-waivable finding, or rejected approval | Repair or escalate and retest |
| REVIEW | Coverage or approvals are incomplete, an exception is expired, scope is too wide, or monitoring or rollback is unready | Obtain the missing evidence or narrow the request |
| APPROVE | All required evidence and human sign-off agree on this version and permitted scope | Proceed only within that scope |

Inspect identity and integrity first, then known blocking failures, then missing prerequisites. An invalid packet cannot authorize release. Independently confirmed severe incidents still need immediate operational escalation. A rejected or unauthorized approval is not usable. Northstar sends an unrecognized approver to REVIEW and treats an explicit rejection from an authorized approver as BLOCK.

### Limit exposure and watch the results

Start with an internal rehearsal. In a shadow evaluation the candidate can propose answers or actions for comparison, but its decisions do not change customer state. Restrict data to approved inputs and isolate tool writes. A canary is a small live exposure. Northstar caps initial approval at 5%, then requires a clean observation window and new scope approval before 25% or full rollout. Small exposure limits the affected population. It cannot eliminate severe effects on the exposed customers.

Monitoring observes real or simulated production signals. An increase in timeouts or missing receipts can be an early warning. A confirmed unauthorized write, privacy disclosure or customer complaint is an outcome observed after the event. The lab uses a minimum 50 canary requests for ordinary rate judgments. An error rate above 4% pauses expansion. A missing receipt pauses independently of rates. One confirmed unauthorized write or private disclosure triggers rollback regardless of the sample size. If the first 100 requests have two errors and complete evidence, keep the canary at 5%. That window gives the approver evidence to consider a 25% request. The original signature still authorizes only 5%.

### Rehearse the return path

If the canary causes an unauthorized refund, stop exposing the candidate and check that northstar-v1 can still use the current policy, tools and data format. Restore its configuration and route, then independently confirm which version is serving. That return to a prior approved version is a rollback. Test the route switch and confirm the restored version with an independent observation. A rollback cannot undo a refund or retract a disclosed value. Contain the affected tool, preserve minimized evidence and let the incident owner decide remediation. If the old version is unsafe or incompatible, pause and isolate the route, then assess a roll-forward to a corrected version through a new review.

An incident is an event that requires investigation. Determine severity from impact and confidence in the evidence. Notify the on-call operator, release owner and security or privacy owner as appropriate. Separate model drift, a behavior or input change over time, from a change to the underlying support policy or measurement. When the policy or evaluator changes, update references and rebaseline rather than comparing incompatible totals. Schedule post-release evaluation and review again after alerts, material changes or incidents. In the retrospective, record the missing test, the owner and the next dataset revision. Governance means keeping those responsibilities and decisions accountable over time.

### Release decision architecture

**Versioned evaluation run → CI quality gate → evidence package → release policy validation → risk and exception review → authorized decision → staged rollout → production monitoring → continue, pause or rollback → audit and follow-up**

| Stage | Required input | Validation | Responsible role | Output | Failure behavior | Evidence retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluation | Frozen manifests and tied receipts | Versions, digest and case coverage | Evaluation engineer | Comparable results | Reject mismatch | Run and case IDs |
| CI | Results and gate policy | Critical cases and coverage | CI runner | Machine status | Route failure or missing data | CI report |
| Package | Candidate, previous version and artifacts | Provenance and completeness | Release owner | Review packet | Gather evidence | Artifact references |
| Policy | Packet and approved release policy | Required clauses and scope | Policy validator | Recommendation | Invalid evidence or review | Version and reasons |
| Risk | Findings and exception requests | Severity, scope, ownership and expiry | Security and risk owners | Risk disposition | Block critical or review unresolved risk | Findings and sign-offs |
| Approval | Valid packet and authorized reviewer | Role, independence, version and scope | Release approver | Signed scope decision | No exposure | Actor, time and scope |
| Rollout | Approved scope and readiness | Target and traffic cap | Release operator | Limited exposure | Keep prior routing | Config and flag change |
| Monitoring | Linked signals and denominators | Freshness, scope and triggers | On-call operator | Observation | Pause on gaps | Window and minimized events |
| Operational action | Trigger and tested return path | Restore and verify, or contain | Incident lead and operator | Continue, pause or rollback | Keep write path contained | Prior and restored versions |
| Follow-up | Outcome, approvals and incident notes | Traceability and access | Release owner | Auditable record and new tests | Keep affected gate closed | Decisions and retained evidence |

### Step 1 — Build a release evidence checklist

A dictionary groups names and their evidence status. `.items()` supplies each name and value to a list comprehension. The condition keeps only missing items.

### Step 2 — Validate a release policy

A policy identifies its approved version and conditions. `==` compares values and `all` requires every condition to hold.

[Open Python Editor](https://www.online-python.com/)

```python
record_version = "release-policy-v1"
policy = {"version": "release-policy-v1", "checks": ["CI", "monitoring", "rollback"]}
record = {"version": record_version, "CI": True, "monitoring": True, "rollback": True}
if record["version"] != policy["version"]:
    result = "INVALID EVIDENCE"
elif all(record[key] for key in policy["checks"]):
    result = "POLICY CHECKS COMPLETE"
else:
    result = "REVIEW"
print("Record policy:", record["version"])
print("Result:", result)
```

```text
Record policy: release-policy-v1
Result: POLICY CHECKS COMPLETE
```

### Step 5 — Plan a staged rollout

List each stage and its required criteria. A set subtraction finds unmet criteria. `break` stops the loop at the first incomplete stage.

[Open Python Editor](https://www.online-python.com/)

```python
monitor_ready = True
ready = {"internal checks", "5% approval"} | ({"monitoring"} if monitor_ready else set())
stages = [("internal", {"internal checks"}), ("5% canary", {"monitoring", "5% approval"}),
          ("25% rollout", {"clean canary", "25% approval"}),
          ("full rollout", {"clean 25% window", "full approval"})]
for name, required in stages:
    gap = sorted(required - ready)
    print(name + ": " + ("WAIT for " + ", ".join(gap) if gap else "READY"))
    if gap:
        break
```

## Real Release Governance Workflow

### Review the release packet

1. Freeze the candidate, previously approved configuration, intended scope and release policy. Retrieve the evaluation and CI reports from their trusted store, validate their versions and digests, and check pairing, coverage, critical cases and receipts.
2. Review open findings, privacy and security controls, retained data and residual risks. Escalate non-waivable defects. Review exception requests with an accountable owner, approval and expiry. Verify that the person approving the release has authority for its scope and is independent where policy requires it.
3. Confirm internal and shadow readiness, the monitored cohort, alert owner, explicit thresholds, runbook, tested return path and target configuration. Generate a recommendation and obtain a version-bound approval. Archive the packet, human decision, reasons, time and permitted exposure.

### Operate and learn

4. Begin the authorized stage. Observe linked canary signals and their denominators. Continue within scope if the window passes. Pause for inadequate evidence or a noncritical threshold breach. For a confirmed critical signal, stop exposure and restore the verified target, or isolate the candidate while the return path is investigated.
5. Keep the incident evidence, triggering window, previous and restored version, operator, notifications and verification of restored behavior. Assess affected writes or disclosures separately.
6. If the candidate changes materially, generate new evaluation evidence and request fresh approval. Update the regression dataset, rebaseline if the policy or evaluator changed, close or renew exceptions through their owner, and record the lessons and monitoring follow-up.

## Knowledge Check Answers

1. Deployment installs or activates a version in an environment. Release makes it available to the intended users. Controls can separate the two steps.
2. CI covers its declared evaluation evidence. Release also requires current findings, compatible evidence, a signed scope, monitoring and a verified rollback plan.
3. It is risk that remains after mitigation. The accountable risk owner documents acceptance, scope, evidence, expiry and follow-up.
4. A confirmed severe authorization or privacy defect cannot be overridden under Northstar’s current policy, regardless of aggregate performance.
5. Bind an independent authorized identity to the release ID, candidate and configuration, evaluation run, policy version, scope and time.
6. It authorizes only the first small exposure. Later stages need their own observation evidence and approval.
7. A confirmed authorization or privacy violation triggers emergency action independently of the ordinary error-rate sample.
8. Keep the old approval, rollback or incident evidence, new candidate, new evaluation and CI reports, closure evidence, revised scope and fresh approval.

## Guided Engineering Lab

### Northstar Release Readiness, Canary Rollout and Rollback Governance

Northstar’s approved version is northstar-v1. A green CI run covers northstar-v2 and prompt-v2. The 24 inline evidence records carry ID, category, severity, version, owner, sequence, source and verification status. The manifest binds the evaluation to the candidate configuration. It reports 18 passes out of 20 planned and completed cases, with zero critical failures in that frozen set. Calculate 18/20 = 90%. The separate F-AUTH finding in Scenario B addresses a path outside those frozen cases.

**Inspect the packet, then predict.** A has no approval. B adds a critical finding and a signature. Neither may expose the candidate. C has independent canary approval. D’s first window has 2/100 errors and the next has 5/100. Only the second is above the 4% threshold. E has a matching incident receipt and ledger change. E pauses customer exposure by restoring the verified northstar-v1 target in the simulation. F uses northstar-v3, prompt-v3 and EVAL-3 with a verified mitigation on the affected path. First it tries the old approval, then it obtains a new one. G exercises expiry exactly at the current sequence. This model treats an exception as expired when the current sequence reaches its expiry.

**Follow the code.** `enumerate(..., 1)` assigns a sequence and `:02d` displays the number with two digits. `deepcopy` creates independent nested records. `canonical` sorts JSON keys so `digest` can fingerprint consistent bytes. An integrity check needs a trusted source for the expected fingerprint. `decide` returns a release result and reason at the first matching rule. `observe` checks the canary separately and only acts on an authorized current candidate and a later window. `archive` keeps a serialized snapshot and checksum per ID, so changing a subsequent approval does not rewrite the earlier packet. The expressions with `:=` assign a named value while passing it to the archive. `assert` checks the dataset count and the old approval outcome.

**Run the tabletop.** Use `no_approval` and `critical` for the first two decisions. Run `approved`, `warning`, `rollback`, `repaired` and `expired` from fresh copies. Check the folded solutions. Set `SHOW_JSON = True` to see full decision records and archived snapshots. The result’s `decision` field is the policy validator’s conclusion from supplied evidence. The `approval` field contains the simulated human decision. Nothing here signs for a person, changes traffic or executes a refund.

**Advance by scope.** The initial canary limit is 5%. Before 25%, obtain a clean authorized canary window, verify monitor and rollback availability again and obtain a named 25% sign-off. Before 100%, repeat those checks on 25% and get full-scope approval. The program simulates the initial gate and canary actions only. Internal rehearsal and shadow comparisons supply evidence for canary entry. No clean window changes a signed traffic limit.

def decide(data):
    manifest, ci = data["manifest"], data["ci"]
    now, scope = data["sequence"], data["scope"]
    records = data["evidence"]
    ids = [row["id"] for row in records]
    expected = {row["id"]: row for row in evidence(data["candidate"])}
    if (data["release_policy_version"] != POLICY["release_policy_version"]
            or scope not in STAGES or data["previous"] != BASELINE
            or any(manifest.get(key) != value for key, value in LOCK.items())
            or manifest.get("config_sha256") != snapshot_key(data)
            or manifest.get("candidate") != data["candidate"]
            or manifest.get("previous") != data["previous"]
            or data["candidate_config"]["release"] != data["candidate"]
            or data["candidate_config"]["policy"] != LOCK["policy_version"]
            or ci.get("run") != manifest.get("id") or ci.get("manifest_sha256") != digest(manifest)
            or ci.get("gate_policy_version") != LOCK["gate_policy_version"]
            or len(ids) != len(set(ids)) or set(ids) - expected.keys()
            or any(row["version"] != data["candidate"] or row["source"] != expected[row["id"]]["source"]
                   or row["sequence"] > now for row in records)):
        return verdict(data, "INVALID EVIDENCE", "release or evaluation provenance mismatch")
    counts = [manifest.get(key) for key in ("passed", "completed", "planned", "critical_failures")]
    if (not all(type(value) is int and value >= 0 for value in counts)
            or not 0 <= counts[0] <= counts[1] <= counts[2] == 20
            or counts[3] > counts[1] - counts[0]
            or ci["status"] not in {"PASS", "BLOCK", "REVIEW"}):
        return verdict(data, "INVALID EVIDENCE", "invalid evaluation counts or CI result")
    critical = [row["id"] for row in data["findings"]
                if row["severity"] == "critical" and not (
                    row["status"] == "verified-closed" and row.get("verified_for") == data["candidate"]
                    and row.get("closure_ref") == "E05"
                    and any(item["id"] == "E05" and item["verified"] and item["status"] == "PASS"
                            for item in records))]
    critical.extend(row["id"] for row in records
                    if row["severity"] == "critical" and row["status"] == "FAIL" and row["verified"])
    if critical and not POLICY["allow_unresolved_critical_findings"]:
        return verdict(data, "BLOCK", "non-waivable critical finding " + ", ".join(critical))
    if manifest["critical_failures"] or (POLICY["require_passing_ci_gate"] and ci["status"] == "BLOCK"):
        return verdict(data, "BLOCK", "critical case or CI gate failed")
    if (POLICY["require_complete_evaluation_evidence"]
            and (manifest["completed"] != manifest["planned"] or set(ids) != set(expected)
                 or any(not row["verified"] or row["status"] != expected[row["id"]]["status"]
                        for row in records))):
        return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "required evidence incomplete")
    if POLICY["require_passing_ci_gate"] and ci["status"] != "PASS":
        return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "CI has no passing decision")
    for item in data["risks"] + data["exceptions"]:
        if item["status"] == "closed":
            continue
        if type(item["expires"]) is not int:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "risk or exception needs an expiry")
        if now >= item["expires"]:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "expired risk or exception " + item["id"])
        if (item["severity"] == "critical" or item["accepted_by"] != RISK_APPROVER
                or item["version"] != data["candidate"] or not item["owner"] or item["evidence"] not in ids
                or item["status"] != "accepted"):
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "risk or exception needs authorized disposition")
    for item in data["exceptions"]:
        if item["status"] == "accepted" and (item["scope"] != scope or not item.get("justification")):
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "exception scope or justification needs review")

    approval = data["approval"]
    if POLICY["require_authorized_approval"]:
        if approval is None:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "authorized sign-off missing")
        role, maximum = APPROVERS.get(approval["person"], ("unknown", 0))
        if role != approval["role"] or role != "release-approver" or approval["person"] == data["author"]:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "signer lacks independent release authority")
        signed = (approval["candidate"], approval["evaluation"], approval["scope"],
                  approval["release_id"], approval["config_sha256"], approval["policy"])
        needed = (data["candidate"], manifest["id"], scope, data["release_id"],
                  snapshot_key(data), data["release_policy_version"])
        if signed != needed or approval["sequence"] > now:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "fresh version- and scope-bound approval required")
        if approval["status"] == "REJECTED":
            return verdict(data, "BLOCK", "authorized reviewer rejected promotion")
        if approval["status"] != "SIGNED" or STAGES[scope] > maximum:
            return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "approval does not cover scope")
    if (data["rollout_plan"]["requested"] != scope or scope == "internal"
            or STAGES[scope] > POLICY["maximum_initial_rollout_percent"]):
        return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "initial rollout exceeds 5% or scope plan differs")
    monitoring, rollback = data["monitoring_plan"], data["rollback_plan"]
    if POLICY["require_monitoring_readiness"] and (
            not monitoring["ready"] or not monitoring["owner"] or monitoring["ref"] not in ids
            or monitoring["minimum_requests"] != POLICY["minimum_window_requests"]
            or monitoring["maximum_error_percent"] != POLICY["maximum_error_percent"]
            or monitoring["critical_events_allowed"] != 0):
        return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "monitoring not verified")
    if POLICY["require_rollback_readiness"] and (
            not rollback["ready"] or not rollback["compatible"] or not rollback["operator"]
            or rollback["ref"] not in ids
            or rollback["target"] != data["previous"] or rollback["target_digest"] != digest(BASE_SNAPSHOT)):
        return verdict(data, "REVIEW", "rollback not verified")
    return verdict(data, "APPROVE", "signed for 5% canary only")

def window(sequence, errors=2, missing=0, violation=None, requests=100):
    return {"id": f"W{sequence}", "sequence": sequence, "candidate": "northstar-v2",
            "scope": "canary", "requests": requests, "errors": errors,
            "missing_receipts": missing, "violation": violation,
            "source": f"fixture://monitor/W{sequence}"}

def observe(approval, active, observation, target):
    result = {"phase": "operations", "sequence": observation["sequence"],
              "active_before": active, "active_after": active, "action": "PAUSE",
              "reason": "no active, authorized canary", "observation": deepcopy(observation),
              "notify": [], "restored_verified": False}
    if (approval["decision"] != "APPROVE" or approval["authorized_scope_percent"] != 5
            or approval["candidate"] != active or observation["candidate"] != active
            or observation["scope"] != "canary" or observation["sequence"] <= approval["sequence"]):
        return result
    violation = observation["violation"]
    if violation is not None:
        confirmed = (violation["verified"] and violation["kind"] in {"authorization", "privacy"}
                     and violation["ref"] == observation["source"] + "/incident"
                     and (violation.get("receipt") == "EXECUTED" and violation.get("ledger_delta") == 1
                          if violation["kind"] == "authorization" else violation.get("disclosed") is True))
        result["notify"] = ["on-call", "release-owner", violation["kind"] + "-owner"]
        plan = approval["rollback_plan"]
        if (confirmed and target["verified"] and target["compatible"]
                and target["active"] == plan["target"] == approval["previous_approved"]
                and target["sha256"] == plan["target_digest"]):
            result.update(action="ROLLBACK", active_after=target["active"], restored_verified=True,
                          reason="confirmed " + violation["kind"] + " violation", restoration=deepcopy(target))
        else:
            result.update(active_after="isolated", reason="isolate candidate while evidence or restoration is unverified")
        return result
    count, errors = observation["requests"], observation["errors"]
    if (type(count) is not int or type(errors) is not int or not 0 <= errors <= count
            or count < POLICY["minimum_window_requests"] or observation["missing_receipts"] != 0):
        result["reason"] = "missing evidence or insufficient observations"
    elif errors * 100 / count > POLICY["maximum_error_percent"]:
        result["reason"] = "error rate above 4%; freeze expansion"
    else:
        result.update(action="CONTINUE", reason="within thresholds; stay at 5%")
    return result

def archive(history, key, value):
    if key in history:
        raise ValueError("duplicate audit key")
    history[key] = (canonical(value), digest(value))

def load_entry(entry):
    payload, checksum = entry
    value = json.loads(payload)
    if digest(value) != checksum:
        raise ValueError("audit digest mismatch")
    return value

MODES = ("no_approval", "critical", "approved", "warning", "rollback",
         "repaired", "expired", "wide_scope", "wrong_evaluator")

def build(mode):
    history = {}
    base = packet()
    archive(history, "A", verdict_a := decide(base))
    if mode == "no_approval":
        return [verdict_a], history
    blocked = packet(31)
    blocked["findings"] = [{"id": "F-AUTH", "severity": "critical", "status": "open",
                            "evidence": "fixture://incident/F-AUTH", "owner": "security"}]
    blocked["approval"] = sign(blocked)
    archive(history, "B", verdict_b := decide(blocked))
    if mode == "critical":
        return [verdict_b], history

    approved = packet(32)
    approved["approval"] = sign(approved)
    canary = decide(approved)
    archive(history, "C", canary)
    if mode == "approved":
        return [canary], history
    target = {"active": BASELINE, "verified": True, "compatible": True,
              "sha256": digest(BASE_SNAPSHOT), "ref": "fixture://restore/northstar-v1"}
    initial = observe(canary, approved["candidate"], window(40), target)
    archive(history, "W40", initial)
    warning = observe(canary, initial["active_after"], window(41, errors=5), target)
    archive(history, "D", warning)
    if mode == "warning":
        return [initial, warning], history
    incident = {"kind": "authorization", "verified": True, "ref": "fixture://monitor/W42/incident",
                "receipt": "EXECUTED", "ledger_delta": 1}
    rolled = observe(canary, warning["active_after"], window(42, errors=5, violation=incident), target)
    archive(history, "E", rolled)
    if mode == "rollback":
        return [rolled], history

    repaired = packet(50, repaired=True)
    repaired["findings"] = [{"id": "F-AUTH", "severity": "critical", "status": "verified-closed",
                             "evidence": incident["ref"], "owner": "security", "closure_ref": "E05",
                             "verified_for": repaired["candidate"], "mitigation": "backend deny enforced"}]
    repaired["approval"] = deepcopy(approved["approval"])
    stale = decide(repaired)
    archive(history, "F-previous", stale)
    if POLICY["require_new_approval_after_material_change"]:
        assert stale["decision"] == "REVIEW"
    repaired["approval"] = sign(repaired)
    fresh = decide(repaired)
    archive(history, "F-fresh", fresh)
    if mode == "repaired":
        return [stale, fresh], history

    expired = packet(60, repaired=True)
    expired["approval"] = sign(expired)
    expired["exceptions"] = [{"id": "EX-1", "severity": "low", "owner": "risk",
                             "justification": "defer advisory wording check", "status": "accepted",
                             "accepted_by": RISK_APPROVER, "expires": 60, "scope": "canary",
                             "version": expired["candidate"], "evidence": "E15"}]
    archive(history, "G", verdict_g := decide(expired))
    if mode == "expired":
        return [verdict_g], history

    extra = packet(61, repaired=True)
    if mode == "wide_scope":
        extra["scope"] = extra["rollout_plan"]["requested"] = "expanded"
        extra["approval"] = sign(extra, "Marin")
    elif mode == "wrong_evaluator":
        extra["manifest"]["evaluator_version"] = "evaluator-v2"
        extra["ci"]["manifest_sha256"] = digest(extra["manifest"])
        extra["approval"] = sign(extra)
    else:
        raise ValueError("choose a documented scenario")
    archive(history, "Assignment", assignment := decide(extra))
    return [assignment], history

def main():
    results, history = build(MODE)
    assert len(EVIDENCE_ROWS) == len({item["id"] for item in evidence("northstar-v2")}) == 24
    print(f"{RELEASE} | {MODE} | 24 evidence records")

                                                           "sha256": entry[1]}

if __name__ == "__main__":

- Verify the exact candidate, approved fallback, policy and comparable evaluation run.
- Confirm coverage, critical results, receipts, privacy controls and current finding status.
- Record accepted risks and time-bound exceptions with ownership and supporting evidence.
- Obtain independent approval for the exact exposure. Confirm alert ownership and rehearse the tested return path.
- Keep the traffic ceiling and kill criteria visible to the operator. Preserve each handoff, observation and approval in the audit.

## Common Mistakes

- If an approval fails after a repair, check the candidate digest, evaluation ID, policy and scope. Obtain a fresh signature.
- If a canary decision seems surprising, check whether the violation is confirmed, the window belongs to the active version and the target is independently verified.
- If evidence is missing, recover the matching records instead of inferring a pass.
- If the audit is long, keep `SHOW_JSON` off for the compact transcript. Turn it on to review the signed scope, receipt and digest.
- If you restore routing, verify system behavior and investigate effects that already occurred.
