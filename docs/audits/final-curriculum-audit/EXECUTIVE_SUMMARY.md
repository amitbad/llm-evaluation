## Readiness assessment

**The course has a strong beginner-to-advanced instructional design. Hold the full public release claim.** All 53 local lesson sources were reviewed and their Python baselines exercised. The public curriculum HTML served during this audit matched commit `97f04a1` byte for byte. It contains 52 full lessons plus an M18-T01 outline. The complete advanced judge lab exists only in pending working-tree changes. The prerequisite graph is identical in both snapshots. [Public Academy curriculum](https://llmacademy.testneural.in/master-curriculum.html) · [Topic scorecard](TOPIC_SCORECARD.md) · [F-001](FINDINGS.md)

**What learners can already do.** The sequence progresses from saved messages, references and small Python checks to versioned benchmarks, judged and independent evidence, statistical comparisons, RAG and tool traces, safety gates and production monitoring. Exercises repeatedly ask learners to predict a result, find a concrete failure and defend an action. M15 requires a reproducible suite, a peer-replayed regression, a stakeholder explanation and an incident recovery recommendation. M16–M18 give real specialization work through controlled fixtures. The course is usable for an evaluation beginner who can use the editor. Learners without terminal or file skills need an explicit practice bridge before the production labs. Independent beginner success and teaching approval have yet to be demonstrated. [Early example](../../lessons/m01-t01.md#L154) · [Capstone rubric](../../lessons/m15-t01.md#L539) · [Decision teach-back](../../lessons/m15-t02.md#L449) · [F-008](FINDINGS.md)

**Red-teaming readiness.** The course teaches an offline defensive assessment. Strong points are the scoped threat model, authorization and receipts that are independent of model wording, multi-surface exposure checks and matched utility controls. Mutation checks exposed three material reporting and evidence-classification findings in M12. A stateful offline defense exercise would close the most important practical transfer gap. The curriculum explicitly limits the existing M12 assessment to authorized fictional scenarios and saved records. [Coverage matrix](RED_TEAMING_COVERAGE.md)

**Experiment readiness.** The audit ran 346 standalone Python blocks using 349 invocations. All 343 ordinary transcripts matched their adjacent documented outputs. Two M14 transcripts also matched their separately located references, and the TC10 replay, repair and intentional lookup failure behaved as documented. Original experiments were separately inventoried and five of their safe components were tested. Historical model output, hosted CI, production tracing, managed evals, browser-editor behavior and learner outcomes remain outside the execution evidence. [Experiment audit](EXPERIMENT_AUDIT.md)

The register contains **one P0, seven P1 and four P2 findings**. The P0 concerns the completeness of the public course. P1 work focuses on M12, independent judge evidence, legacy terminology and the move into terminal work. The audit identified no reason to add a full module. [Prioritized register](FINDINGS.md)

## Technical review highlights

| Area | What the audit found |
|---|---|
| Accuracy, precision, recall and F1 | M05-T01 explicitly sets the unit to tokens when measuring overlap. M17-T01 uses class and extracted-value counts and shows how a wrong nonempty field contributes misses on both sides. The micro-F1 equivalence is limited to complete single-label multiclass cases. [Token metrics](../../lessons/m05-t01.md#L19) · [Classification metrics](../../lessons/m17-t01.md#L39) |
| BLEU, ROUGE and embedding similarity | Sentence and corpus aggregation, multiple references, clipped counts, a length penalty, and the difference between word order and meaning are correctly separated in the inspected comparisons. Cosine goes to review when a vector is missing or has zero norm. A high score does not establish policy compliance. [BLEU and ROUGE lesson](../../lessons/m05-t01.md#L30) · [Embedding lesson](../../lessons/m05-t02.md#L210) · [Original BLEU paper](https://aclanthology.org/P02-1040/) · [Original ROUGE paper](https://aclanthology.org/W04-1013/) |
| Agreement, kappa and judge validation | The case examples distinguish policy correctness from agreement and handle undefined kappa at a saturated marginal. The observed 0.75 agreement and 0.625 chance baseline independently reproduce κ = 0.3333. M18-T01 makes a useful distinction between reviewer alignment and probability calibration. Its main validation lab needs independent predictions. [Kappa calculation](../../lessons/m06-t02.md#L53) · [Calibration](../../lessons/m18-t01.md#L212) · [F-006](FINDINGS.md) |
| Confidence intervals, significance, paired testing and practical effect | Independently confirmed the 8/10 Wilson interval of 49.02%–94.33% and two-sided exact McNemar p = 0.030883789 for 4 versus 14 discordant cases. The lesson distinguishes the marginal interval, paired bootstrap and exact test, and states independent case-unit and coverage assumptions. M07-T03 separates a noninferiority margin from a nonsignificant equality test and distinguishes randomized assignment from exposure. [M07-T02](../../lessons/m07-t02.md#L45) · [M07-T03](../../lessons/m07-t03.md#L54) · [Exact McNemar reference](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.contingency_tables.mcnemar.html) |
| RAG, tools, safety and privacy | Correctness, context sufficiency, source support, trust, authorization and observed effects have dedicated checks. The strong practice is comparing ledgers and receipts with the final reply. Treat historical Test 9 claims and archived framework aggregates as reported grader results. M12 needs the evidence corrections in F-002 through F-005. [RAG evidence](../../lessons/m08-t02.md#L97) · [Trace outcomes](../../lessons/m10-t02.md#L672) · [F-007](FINDINGS.md) |
| Reproducibility, migration and research | M04 protects reserved families and labels. M13 binds run identity, compatible inputs and release authority. M18 separates overlap signals from proven training exposure, replay from replication on new cases, and a scoped pilot from production approval. M18-T03 distinguishes targeted failure discovery from rate estimation. [M04-T03](../../lessons/m04-t03.md#L358) · [M13-T01](../../lessons/m13-t01.md#L536) · [M18-T02](../../lessons/m18-t02.md#L193) · [M18-T03](../../lessons/m18-t03.md#L149) · [Active Testing paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/kossen21a.html) |

The review did not find a confirmed formula error in the sampled arithmetic for these core metrics. It did find program behaviors that conflict with their intended evidence interpretation. Reproduced baseline numbers alone would not have exposed those behaviors. The course’s use of Harbor Leaf and Northstar does not materially break continuity. Verify the named policy snapshot and cutoff when examples switch from delivery-based to purchase-based refund rules. Case, CI and governance terms merit a short crosswalk. [F-011](FINDINGS.md)
