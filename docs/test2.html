What We Did

Ran three different types of tasks on all 4 models, 3 times each — to see how consistent they are and how quality varies across task types. Then manually scored each output on a 1–5 scale for coherence, accuracy, and other qualities.

Consistency Finding

Key insight: The type of task determines how consistent results are. Creative tasks vary a lot — that is expected and by design. Factual and structured tasks are reliable. This is important when designing your own test cases: do not judge a creative task for inconsistency, and do not accept inconsistency in a factual task.

Key Learnings

  • All 4 models ran without crashing. Basic stability confirmed across task types — a good starting baseline.
  • Mistral is the fastest, deepseek the most thorough. Choose a model based on what matters more for your use case — speed or depth.
  • Manual scoring gives you a number, but numbers need context. A score of 4/5 for coherence means nothing without knowing what 4 looks like versus 3. This is why structured criteria matter in evaluation.
  • Three runs reveal variance, one run hides it. If you only run a test once, you cannot tell if the result was typical or an outlier. Run at least 3 times for anything you care about.
