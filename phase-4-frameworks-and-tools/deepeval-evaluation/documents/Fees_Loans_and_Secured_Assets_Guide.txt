This document serves as the definitive schedule of charges and secured lending terms for NextGen Banking and Finance for the 2025/2026 fiscal year. All service fees are debited on the first business day of the month following the activity.

Our service charge structure is designed for transparency. The standard Monthly Maintenance Fee is £12.00, which is waived entirely for customers who maintain a Minimum Monthly Balance of £2,500 or have a total loan liability exceeding £15,000. Overdraft facilities are provided as a "Buffer" service; the first £50 of an arranged overdraft is interest-free. Beyond this, a fee of £1.50 per day applies for arranged usage, while unarranged overdrafts incur a penalty of £8.00 per day, capped at £80.00 per calendar month. Failed payment fees are £15.00 per occurrence. For global operations, NextGen charges a flat Foreign Remittance fee of £12.00 for outbound SWIFT transfers and £4.00 for SEPA transfers. Inbound foreign transfers are free, but a 2.5% "Currency Conversion Margin" is applied to the mid-market rate.

NextGen offers specialized Loan Against Securities (LAS) and Gold Loans for immediate liquidity. For LAS, the loan value is determined by the asset class: Shares and Equity Mutual Funds allow for a draw-down of up to 50% of the pledged value, capped at £20,000. Debt Mutual Funds offer a higher limit of 80% of the pledged value. Fixed Maturity Plans (FMPs) require a minimum loan of £1,000, while Sovereign Gold Bonds and Life Insurance Policies require a minimum of £500. Our Gold Loans are available to citizens aged 18-85, offering instant disbursal of up to £2,000,000 against gold jewellery. The interest rate for these secured products ranges from 8.75% to 16.65% per annum, with a processing fee of up to 2% of the loan amount plus applicable taxes.

We provide four flexible repayment options for secured loans: a "Bullet Repayment" at the end of the tenure, standard monthly principal and interest payments, an Overdraft facility where interest is only charged on the withdrawn amount, and a "Flexi-Pay" option which allows for repayment without foreclosure charges. Unlike standard personal loans, Gold Loan eligibility does not heavily weight credit scores or employment status, as the physical gold acts as the primary security. Regarding taxation, under the UK Foreign Income and Gains (FIG) regime, funds remitted through the Temporary Repatriation Facility (TRF) are subject to a fixed 12% tax rate.
