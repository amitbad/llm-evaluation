The NextGen LearnFund and Credit suite provides specialized credit facilities for education and lifestyle management. These products are governed by the Consumer Credit Act and require specific eligibility evidence.

The NextGen Credit Card carries a standard purchase rate of 19.9% Variable APR. There is no annual fee, but a "Late Payment Penalty" of £12.00 is applied if the minimum payment (the greater of £5 or 3% of the balance) is not received by the due date. Cash advances on the card incur a 3% fee (minimum £3.00) and interest is charged from the date of withdrawal. For specialized education, the LearnFund provides capital for AI-specific courses. Beyond university degrees, we fund professional certifications from the following approved providers: DeepSkill Academy, NeuralPath Institute, CogniLearn Systems, Algorithmic Edge, Synapse Training Co., and TensorPro Education.

Loan amounts range from £500 to £30,000. Interest rates differ by qualification type: accredited university degrees and postgraduate programmes in AI, Machine Learning, or Data Science are financed at 3.2% APR. Professional certifications and short courses from approved providers are financed at 6.1% APR. The following are explicitly not eligible for LearnFund financing: bootcamps or intensive programmes shorter than 3 months in duration, self-paced courses without a formal assessment or graded examination, and any course or provider not appearing on the NextGen approved list. A repayment holiday (deferment) applies until 30 days after course completion. Documentation required for a LearnFund application includes a formal "Letter of Acceptance" from an approved provider and three months of payslips proving a minimum income of £1,500 per month. If a borrower wishes to "Foreclose" or settle their LearnFund early, they must pay the outstanding principal plus a flat £50.00 administrative discharge fee. Applications are processed through the NextGen Online Portal, with a dedicated "Education Support Liaison" assigned to each applicant during the 3–5 day underwriting window. Branch-specific services for LearnFund are limited to original document verification and high-value disbursement consultations (for loans over £20,000).
