NextGen Banking and Finance operates as a digital-first institution with specific physical branch services available for complex wealth management. This guide outlines the mandatory operational procedures for account maintenance, opening, and lifecycle management.

To open any NextGen account, applicants must provide two distinct documents: a primary ID (valid UK Passport, EU National ID Card, or Full UK Driving Licence) and a secondary proof of address dated within the last 90 days (Utility bill, Council Tax statement, or HMRC correspondence). The account opening process is initiated via the NextGen Mobile Portal, featuring a mandatory "Liveness Check" biometrics scan, and typically results in a decision within 2 business hours. Upon approval, a Contactless Visa Debit ATM Card is dispatched via first-class post, arriving within 3–5 working days. This card carries a daily ATM withdrawal limit of £500 for Standard accounts and £1,000 for Platinum accounts.

Maintaining account activity is critical; an account is classified as "Inactive" if no customer-initiated transactions occur for 12 consecutive months. After 24 months of inactivity, the account is designated "Dormant." To reactivate a dormant account, the holder must submit a "Form D-1" along with fresh KYC (Know Your Customer) documentation and a physical signature verification at a partner branch. For those looking to leave NextGen, we adhere to the Current Account Switch Service (CASS) Guarantee, ensuring all balances and direct debits are transferred to another bank within 7 working days. Account closure or "Foreclosure" of a linked savings product is free of charge, provided the account is not in a negative balance.
